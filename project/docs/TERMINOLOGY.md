# ConstructPM — Terminology Reference

> Last updated: May 2026
> This is the authoritative glossary for the codebase. When writing new code, docs, or having conversations about the platform, use these terms exactly as defined.

---

## 1. The Three-Tier Hierarchy

The most important distinction. These three terms refer to different relationships and must never be conflated.

| Term | Definition | DB table | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Firm** | The contracting company running the platform. Pat's client. There is exactly **one firm per ConstructPM deployment**. The firm employs all the users and operates the platform. | (none — implicit) | The NJ-based electrical contracting company |
| **Customer** | The firm's clients. The people who hire the firm to do work. | `customers` | Turner Construction, Skanska, NYC Department of Education, a building owner |
| **Location** | A physical jobsite. Where work happens. Has an address, town, state, local union, miles from HQ. | `locations` | "100 Federal St, Newark NJ" — a specific construction site |

**Hierarchy:** Firm → Customer → Location → Bid → Project

A bid joins one customer with one location ("Turner is having us bid on the Newark site"). Once won, the bid becomes a project.

**Common conflation to avoid:** never use "customer" when you mean "the firm." That's the exact ambiguity the platform exists to disambiguate.

## 2. People (Roles)

The platform has six distinct roles. Each user has exactly one role. Permissions and visible tabs differ per role.

| Role | DB enum value | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | `admin` | Full system access. Manages users, rate sheets, global variables, templates, audit log. Can do anything. |
| **Project Manager (PM)** | `project_manager` | Owns bids and projects. Creates bids, runs quoting, marks bids won. Verifies POs. Records invoice payments. Sees only their own projects (unless explicitly granted access). |
| **Estimator** | `estimator` | Creates bids and runs quoting. Can hand off bids to a PM (via `assigned_pm_id` on the bid). Sees their originated projects read-only after the PM takes over. |
| **Accounting** | `accounting` | Read-only access to all projects. Runs CSV exports for QuickBooks/Procore. Verifies invoices. |
| **Shop Staff** | `shop_staff` | Equipment manager. Checks equipment in/out, fulfills equipment requests, manages inventory. Sees the kiosk display board. |
| **Foreman** | `foreman` | Field staff. Mobile-only access (no web UI). Submits timesheets, oil samples, and field notes via the React Native app. Sees only projects they're assigned to. |

**Common conflation to avoid:** "PM" is short for Project Manager, not for the platform itself. Don't say "the PM tab" when you mean "the dashboard." A PM is a person; the dashboard is a screen.

## 3. Documents (Things People Generate)

Different document types flow through different inboxes and workflows. Don't mix them up.

| Term | DB table | Generated when | Who creates it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bid** | `bids` | Estimator/PM creates a quote for a customer | Estimator or PM |
| **Quote** | `bid_quote_lines` | A bid's pricing detail — classification × hours × locked rates | (auto from bid creation) |
| **Project** | `projects` | A bid is marked won | (auto when bid wins) |
| **Invoice** | `invoices` | The firm bills the customer for completed work | Generated by PM, sent to customer |
| **Purchase Order (PO)** | `purchase_orders` | The firm orders materials/services from a vendor | Generated by PM |
| **Timesheet** | `timesheets` | Foreman records hours worked per week | Foreman (mobile) |
| **Field Note** | `field_notes` | Foreman records on-site observation | Foreman (mobile) |
| **Oil Sample** | `oil_sample_requests` | Foreman submits an electrical equipment fluid sample | Foreman (mobile) |
| **Contract** | `contracts` | Customer-facing contract document filed against a project | Uploaded post-bid-won |

**A note on Invoice vs PO direction:**
- Invoice = the firm bills *out*. Money flows in to the firm.
- PO = the firm orders *in* (from a vendor). Money flows out from the firm.

Both are "financials" but they're opposites. Don't lump them.

## 4. Identifiers (Numbers)

The platform creates several different number formats. Each has a specific meaning.

| Identifier | Format | Created when | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bid Number** | `YY-PMInitials-Seq` (e.g. `26-MT-014`) | At bid creation | Internal tracking of bids |
| **Primary Project Number** | `<PMCode><YY>-<LocCode>.<Seq>` (e.g. `M26-1308.1`) | At bid-won (project creation) | The canonical project number — what foremen type, what shows on POs and invoices |
| **Supplement Project Number (SPN)** | Free-form (e.g. `2026-MT-001`, customer's PO ref, etc.) | Added manually post-creation | Secondary identifiers for one project (customer PO ref, state grant ref, etc.) |
| **PM Code** | 1-2 char letter code (e.g. `M` for Mike) | When a user is assigned PM role | Embeds in project numbers; identifies the owning PM |
| **Location Code** | Numeric, admin-assigned (e.g. `1308`) | When admin creates a location | Embeds in project numbers; identifies the jobsite |
| **PO Number** | `<ProjectNumber>-PO-<Seq>` (e.g. `M26-1308.1-PO-002`) | When PM creates a PO | Vendor-facing PO reference |
| **Invoice Number** | Per-firm convention, often `<ProjectNumber>-<Seq>` | When PM creates an invoice | Customer-facing invoice reference |

**Important — primary vs SPN:**
- A project has **exactly one** Primary number. That's the canonical identifier.
- A project has **zero or more** SPNs. Those are alternate references.
- When the docs say "the project number," they mean the **Primary** unless otherwise specified.

## 5. Statuses (State Machines)

Each major record type has a status enum. These are the only valid values.

### Bid status (`bid_status`)
- `draft` — being edited by estimator/PM
- `submitted` — sent to customer
- `pending` — awaiting customer response
- `won` — accepted by customer (auto-creates a project)
- `lost` — rejected by customer
- `archived` — old/inactive bid kept for reference
- `cancelled` — cancelled before submission

### Project status (`project_status`)
- `active` — work in progress
- `on_hold` — paused, may resume
- `completed` — closed out
- `cancelled` — terminated before completion

### Invoice status (`invoice_status`)
- `pending` — drafted, not yet sent
- `approved` — sent to customer
- `paid` — payment received in full
- `partial_paid` — partial payment received
- `overdue` — past `payment_due_date`
- `cancelled` — voided

### PO status (`po_status`)
- `draft` — being created
- `submitted` — sent to vendor
- `received` — materials received
- `partial` (or `partially_received`) — some line items received
- `cancelled` — voided

### Equipment status (`equipment_status`)
- `available` — in shop, ready to check out
- `checked_out` — assigned to a project
- `maintenance_required` — flagged but not yet serviced
- `in_maintenance` — actively being serviced
- `retired` — out of service permanently

### Equipment request status (`equipment_request_status`)
- `open` — PM created the request, shop hasn't filled
- `partially_filled` — some items assigned, more pending
- `filled` — all items assigned
- `cancelled` — request voided

### Contract type (`contract_type`)
- `contract` — fixed-price contract job
- `t_and_m` — time & materials (display: **T&M**)

### Extraction status (`extraction_status`) — for AI-pipeline pending records
- `processing` — AI is working
- `pending` — awaiting user verification
- `confirmed` — user verified, written to target table
- `rejected` — user rejected
- `failed` — AI couldn't extract anything usable

### Notification category
- `actionable` — requires user action; can't be dismissed
- `informational` — FYI; can be dismissed individually or via "Dismiss All"

## 6. AI Pipeline Terms

Terms specific to the OCR + AI extraction flow.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Extraction** | A pending OCR + AI parse of a user-uploaded document, awaiting verification |
| **Verification** | User reviews extracted fields and confirms / corrects them |
| **Confirmation** | Final step — verified data written to the target table (timesheets, invoices, etc.) |
| **Vendor Profile** | Per-vendor learning data — corrections users have made get saved as hints for future extractions from the same vendor |
| **Confidence** | Per-field score (0-100%) representing AI's certainty. Below ~40% triggers fallback to Claude API. |

## 7. Storage / Folders

The filesystem layout has specific names.

| Path | What's stored |
|---|---|
| `bids/<PMInitials><Seq>_<Location>_<Scope>/` | A bid's working folder — quote spreadsheet, generated docs |
| `projects/<year>/<PM>/<Customer>/<Name>/` | A project's main folder, created on bid-won |
| `projects/.../invoices/` | Invoices filed under this project |
| `projects/.../timesheets/` | Timesheets filed under this project |
| `projects/.../purchase_orders/` | POs filed under this project |
| `projects/.../Contract_PO/` | The customer-facing contract document for this project |
| `inbox/<type>/` | Pre-extraction landing zone for uploaded docs |
| `templates/bid/` | Per-PM bid Word/Excel templates |
| `equipment_docs/<id>/` | Equipment certification documents |
| `exports/` | Generated CSV/XLSX exports |

## 8. Notification Types (the 13)

Each is either actionable or informational. Routing varies.

| # | Trigger | Goes to | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Timesheet uploaded to inbox | PM + delegate | actionable |
| 2 | Invoice uploaded (matched) | PM delegate or admins | actionable |
| 3 | PO uploaded (matched) | PM | actionable |
| 4 | Invoice/PO not matched | Admins | actionable |
| 5 | Timesheet confirmed | PM | informational |
| 6 | Contract uploaded | PM | actionable |
| 7 | Bid won → project created | Admin + PM + delegate | informational |
| 8 | Bid inactive (configurable days) | PM | actionable |
| 9 | Contract_PO empty after 1 day | PM | actionable |
| 10 | Invoice payment overdue | PM + delegate | actionable (escalates) |
| 11 | Revenue ≥ contract value | PM | actionable |
| 12 | Equipment request created | Shop staff + select users | actionable |
| 13 | Equipment request filled | Requesting PM | informational |

## 9. Things That Are NOT What You Might Think

These are sneaky. Worth calling out.

| Term | What it does NOT mean | What it DOES mean |
|---|---|---|
| **PM Code** | Project Management Code | The 1-2 letter abbreviation for a specific Project Manager (e.g. `M` for Mike Torres) |
| **Local Union** | A location concept | An attribute on a `location`, e.g. "Local 164" — drives which `rate_sheet` rates apply |
| **Per Diem** | A subtype of mileage | Daily allowance paid to workers; not subject to markup; pass-through cost only |
| **Mileage** | Auto-calculated from Google Maps | Stored on the location; user enters manually (Google Maps integration is TODO) |
| **Rate Sheet** | A document | The DB table holding union rates (`rate_sheet`) — local × classification × ST/OT/DT |
| **Quote** | A bid | A bid's *line items* (`bid_quote_lines`) — the classification × hours × rates breakdown |
| **Inbox** | A user's notification list | The pre-extraction upload zones (timesheets, invoices, POs) — separate from notifications |
| **Display Board** | A page in the SPA | A standalone kiosk page (`display.html`) for shop floor TV — no login, separate from main app |
| **Active Projects** | All projects with status='active' | A specific dashboard view that adds weekly hours, oil samples, and field notes per project |
| **Estimator** | A type of bid (rough estimate) | A user role that creates bids and hands them off to PMs |
| **Field Notes** | Bid quoting comments | Foreman observations submitted from the mobile app, stored in `field_notes` |
| **Form Templates** | Bid templates | Specifically oil-sample form templates (admin-controlled, no defaults) — different from `bid_templates` |

## 10. Things With Multiple Names (Acceptable Synonyms)

Sometimes the codebase uses two terms for the same thing. These are equivalent:

| Variation 1 | Variation 2 | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Project Manager | PM | The role / person |
| Time & Materials | T&M | A contract type |
| Purchase Order | PO | A document |
| Foreman | Field Staff | The mobile-only role (legacy code uses `field_staff` in places; `foreman` is preferred) |
| Bid | Quote | When referring to the document the customer sees. (Internally, "bid" is the record and "quote" is its line items.) |
| Inbox | Centralized Inbox | The upload landing zones |
| Project Number | Primary number | When unqualified, "project number" means the Primary |
| Cert | Certification | Equipment licensing/inspection documentation |

## 11. Things That Sound Similar But Are Different

| Pair | First | Second |
|---|---|---|
| `customers` vs **the customer** | DB table of the firm's clients | Don't use this phrase to mean "the firm" |
| Customer Contact vs Customer | A person at a company | The company itself |
| `customer_contact_id` (on a bid) vs `customer_id` | Specific person at the company | The company |
| Project vs Bid | Won bids that became projects | Pre-win quote |
| PO vs Invoice | Outgoing (firm pays vendor) | Incoming (customer pays firm) |
| `assigned_pm_id` vs `estimator_id` (on a bid) | Who owns it post-win | Who created it |
| `pm_id` (on a project) vs `assigned_pm_id` (on the source bid) | Same value, but separate field on the project record | Same person, but recorded on the bid |
| Pending Extraction vs Pending Notification | Awaiting AI completion | Awaiting user dismissal |
| Bid Number vs Project Number | YY-PMInitials-Seq | PMCode+YY-LocCode.Seq |
| Mileage vs Per Diem | Per-mile reimbursement | Per-day daily allowance |
| Rate Sheet vs Quote Lines | Master pay rates | Locked snapshot for a specific bid |
| `inbox_access` vs Inbox | DB table for who can view what inbox | The inbox itself |

## How to use this doc

When you (or anyone working on the platform) is unsure whether to use a term, look it up here. If a term you're using regularly isn't on the list, propose adding it. If two terms in the codebase mean the same thing, decide which is preferred and update Section 10.

When writing new docs or commit messages, use these terms exactly. The platform exists to disambiguate construction project management — the docs should match.
